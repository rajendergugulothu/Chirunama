## What is Chirunama?

In Warangal, Hanamkonda and Kazipet, finding a home still means scrolling WhatsApp groups, Facebook posts and newspaper classifieds, with no way to tell a genuine listing from a stale one. **Chirunama brings every rental, sale, plot and shop into one trusted, searchable place**, in Telugu and English.

> **Verified listings. Direct contact. Your language.**

---

## How It Works

```
  Owner or broker lists a property
         │
         ▼
  Checks are recorded on the listing
  (owner verified · documents checked · site visited)
         │
         ▼
  Buyers and tenants search by locality, budget and type
         │
         ▼
  One tap to WhatsApp or call the owner or broker directly
         │
         ▼
  Every share link is tagged, so brokers see where each lead came from
```

---

## Key Features

- 🏠 **Every property type** — rentals, sales, plots and commercial space in one place
- 🔍 **Smart search** — filter by locality, budget, BHK and furnishing, or show only owners or verified listings
- ✅ **Transparent trust badges** — each badge shows exactly what was checked, by whom and when
- 🗣️ **Telugu-first** — every page in Telugu and English, with a one-tap switch
- 💬 **Direct contact** — WhatsApp and call buttons, no middle layer
- 👤 **Broker profiles** — a shareable page that works as the broker's own website
- 📊 **Broker dashboard** — leads by stage and source, listing performance and a share kit
- 📰 **Print-ready classifieds** — Telugu newspaper ads generated from each listing
- 📍 **Locality pages** — average prices and live listings for each neighbourhood
- 📱 **Mobile-first** — designed for phones first, with a web app manifest

## Tech Stack

**Frontend** — Next.js 16 (App Router) · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS 4 · Noto Sans Telugu

**Data** — PostgreSQL · PostGIS · Prisma 7 · Zod validation

**Quality** — Vitest · ESLint · strict TypeScript

---

## Pages

- **Home** — browse by category, popular localities and the latest listings
- **Search** — filterable results for every category
- **Listing** — details, trust badges and direct contact
- **Broker profile** — the broker's live listings and contact buttons
- **Locality** — neighbourhood price averages and listings
- **Dashboard** — the broker's leads pipeline, listing performance and share kit

Server-rendered for fast loads and search visibility, with a layout that works from a 390px phone up to a desktop.
